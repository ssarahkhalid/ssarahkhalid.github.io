about our practice

we are a creative product studio for AI/ML — working at the intersection of product development, alignment research, and culture. named for the explorative mode of dialogue and the process of transformation through language, we build systems and experiences that respond to genuine needs while facilitating meaningful connection.

our specialty is product management for applied AI/ML research: helping teams turn nascent capabilities into products that work in the real world, with the safety architecture and evaluation discipline that high-stakes deployment demands.

we operate as a full product studio — partnering with engineers, designers, UX researchers, data scientists, and other practitioners to deliver end-to-end across R&D, product development, and go-to-market. our work also extends to coaching, narrative writing, cultural branding, and community building.
